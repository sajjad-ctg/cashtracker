Privacy Policy

Cash Tracker · by MSKCTG · last updated 11 November 2026

No data collection · No tracking · No ads

Cash Tracker is a completely offline app. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information — which is: nothing.

What the app stores

The entries you create (amount, date, category, the optional “for whom” name and note) plus your preferences (theme, currency, categories, week start) are stored only on your own device, inside the app's private storage space (localStorage). They are never uploaded anywhere.

What the app never does

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no phone number.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs.
  • No cookies and no tracking identifiers.
  • No network requests at all — the app does not even request internet access.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Permissions

The Android app requests one permission, limited to devices running Android 9 or older: WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE. It is used solely so the files you export (PDF report, CSV, JSON backup) can be written into your Downloads folder. On Android 10 and newer no permission is required at all — the app writes through the system media store. The app never reads files you did not export yourself.

Data you export

Reports, CSV files and JSON backups are written to your device's storage at your request. Once a file is exported it is an ordinary file that you own: you can open it, share it, or delete it. The app has no knowledge of what happens to it afterwards.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Data → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local storage. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing else to delete.

Backups

If you want to move your entries to another device, export a JSON backup and import it there. The backup file contains your entries in plain text — treat it like any other personal document.

Open source

The full source code is available on GitHub, so the behaviour described here can be verified by anyone: github.com/sajjad-ctg/cashtracker.

Contact
